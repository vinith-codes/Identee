📏 How to measure

Lay the garment flat on a table, front up, no stretching. Body sizes in inches, print sizes in cm.

A. The garment (size chart)

  • Shoulder: seam to seam, straight across.
  • Chest (round): across, just under the arms, then × 2 (all the way round).
  • Length: from the highest point of the shoulder, next to the collar, down to the hem.

B. Where a print sits (print team)

  • ① Start line: from the collar seam straight down to the top edge of the print, in cm.
  • ② From the centre: from the centre line to the nearest edge of a chest print, in cm.
  • "Wearer's left" = the left side for the person wearing it (your right when you look at it).
